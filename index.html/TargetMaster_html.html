Target Master Rules & Theory

Binomial Distribution Mathematics

1. Game Rules

  • Each player receives exactly 10 throwing attempts.
  • Player decides in advance how many to allocate: s + r = 10.
  • Safe Target: Success probability p = 0.60, Reward: 1 point.
  • Risky Target: Success probability p = 0.20, Reward: 4 points.
  • Equal final scores are recorded as ties (no sudden death).

2. Binomial Distribution Model

Because every attempt has 2 independent outcomes (Success or Failure) with constant probability:

S ~ Binomial(s, 0.60)  and  R ~ Binomial(r, 0.20)

Total Score formula: Total Score = 1·S + 4·R

Probability of exactly k successes: P(X = k) = C(n,k)·p^k·(1-p)^(n-k)

3. Expected Value (EV) Strategies

Expected score per attempt = probability × reward:

  • Safe: 0.60 × 1 = 0.60 pts / throw → 10 Safe: 6.00 pts
  • Risky: 0.20 × 4 = 0.80 pts / throw → 10 Risky: 8.00 pts
  • Balanced: 5 Safe (3.0) + 5 Risky (4.0) → 7.00 pts

Note: While 10 Risky has the highest expected value (8.00 pts), it also has the highest variance: there is an 10.74% chance of scoring 0 points! Safe strategy provides reliability and a higher floor.
